## D-007 · 2026-11-16 · M1 · kill -9 test (part 1: objects)
- **Setup:** `spike/killtest` (signed with the Apple Development identity). A tone process is tapped (`mutedWhenTapped`) by a second process with an aggregate on the built-in speakers. The tapping process gets SIGKILL; a third process lists, and sweeps, taps and aggregates.
- **Result (macOS 27.2):** with `isPrivate = false`, the tap and aggregate **survive SIGKILL as orphans**, and another process can see and destroy them (`AudioHardwareDestroy*` → 0). With `isPrivate = true`, the tap and aggregate are **invisible to other processes**, both while running and after SIGKILL. Capture permission was present (tap delivered non-zero input).
- **Part 2 (by ear, owner, AirPods, `spike/killtest/listen_test.sh`):** private tap, after SIGKILL: **tone came back**. Public orphan, after SIGKILL and before sweep: **tone came back**. After sweep: tone present.
- **Conclusion:** the mute of `mutedWhenTapped` is tied to the **live tapping process/client**, not to the existence of the tap object. When the process dies, macOS restores the original audio by itself, even if the public tap object lingers. → **Plan A holds:** keep `isPrivate = true` taps and aggregates; **no guard helper** (Plan B dropped, and it could not have seen private objects anyway). A launch-time sweep is only a defensive clean-up of public leftovers with our prefix (not needed for audio, only for tidiness). The watchdog's last resort (exit + relaunch) is safe, because process death unmutes.
- Acoustic loopback via the built-in mic was not possible: the CLI host has no microphone permission, so the mic delivers exact zeros.

## D-012 · 2026-11-16 · M2 · DSP design changes from the review
- **Leveler → feed-forward:** loudness is measured on the input *before* the AGC gain (no feedback loop, so no limit cycle). The compressor make-up is referenced at target − 2.3 dB (per-channel RMS of a centred signal at the LUFS target). A slow trim loop on the post-compressor loudness corrects the remainder: 0.9 /s nominal (≈ 0.3 /s through 3:1), ±4 dB, anti-windup (integrates only while the AGC is within 1 dB of its target and not pinned at ±12 dB). The pause gate leaks down 0.5 dB per block after 3 s of pause (no latch-up).
- **Limiter:** sliding minimum (evict before push, cap la+3) → box average over la+1 → gain ≤ requirement by construction (attack exact, no clipping); the release is a one-pole of 50 ms. The requirement is always computed (warm history); toggling HS_P_LIMIT_ON blends the gain over 10 ms. The final clamp only catches rounding, and a counter (`limiterClips`) asserts it stays 0.
- **Constant latency:** the limiter's look-ahead delay stays in the path whenever any stage is active, so chain latency = 72 samples at 48 kHz when active, 0 for "aus" (bit-identical). While fully bypassed, the limiter runs warm on a scratch copy. **Trade-off:** switching from "aus" to an active preset crossfades over 10 ms, during which the dry part is the unprocessed original that was already playing (it can exceed −1 dBFS for those 10 ms). Accepted: no new risk compared with "aus".
- **Robustness:** non-finite input samples → 0; a non-finite output block resets all stages, is replaced by silence and is counted (`dspResets`). FTZ is set on the I/O thread. `set_param` rejects NaN and clamps ranges. `process_planar` chunks by maxFrames. The de-esser smoothing is rate-independent.

## D-016 · 2026-11-16 · M4 · DeepFilterNet3 integration
